## Identity
- **Full name:** Ibrahim Adepoju Adeyanju (official GBB profile; some outlets misspell "Adebayo")
- **Title:** Professor of Computer Engineering (Intelligent Systems); Managing Director/CEO, Galaxy Backbone Limited (GBB)
- **Appointed MD/CEO:** 23 February / March 2024, by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu
- **Prior GBB role:** Executive Director, Digital Exploration & Technical Services (April 2023)

## Education
| Years | Qualification | Institution |
|---|---|---|
| 1999–2004 | B.Tech. Computer Engineering, **First Class** | LAUTECH, Ogbomoso |
| 2006–2007 | M.Sc. Computing Information Engineering | Robert Gordon University, Aberdeen |
| 2007–2011 | Ph.D. Computing (AI/ML/NLP) | Robert Gordon University, Aberdeen |
| post-PhD | EPSRC-sponsored postdoc, information retrieval | Robert Gordon University |
| 2014 | MIT Empowering the Teachers (ETT) Fellowship | MIT, USA |

## Academic career
- Lecturer at LAUTECH; research posts at RGU and MIT
- Joined Federal University Oye-Ekiti (FUOYE) 2012; rose to Professor of Intelligent Systems, Dept. of Computer Engineering
- Pioneer Director of Quality Assurance, FUOYE
- ~970+ Google Scholar citations (profile: Z97RmFAAAAAJ)
- Research: AI, machine learning, NLP, information retrieval, pattern recognition, Yoruba handwriting corpus/character recognition, automated grading, embedded/microprocessor systems

## Memberships & personal honours
- COREN-registered Computer Engineer
- Fellow, Nigerian Young Academy (NYA)
- Stephen Awokoya Scholarship for Science Education; PTDF scholarship; UK ORSAS award; MIT-ETT fellowship
- Named among Top 100 leading personalities in Nigerian telecoms
- Blueprint Newspapers 2025 Enhanced Digital Infrastructure Award

## Galaxy Backbone under his leadership (2024–2026)
- Strategy: Integrated Digital Transformation Strategy (IDTS) 2023–2028; Government-as-a-Platform (GaaP)
- **1Government Cloud** expansion; data sovereignty agenda; planned data centres across geopolitical zones
- **GovMail** — locally developed email for 100,000+ federal professionals
- **Project 774** — broadband to underserved LGAs (9 connected so far)
- **Fibre-to-Hostel** at UNILAG, UniAbuja, UniJos; connectivity to Nnamdi Azikiwe Int'l Airport & Federal Secretariats
- WIOCC partnership for national fibre backbone; fibre across ~30 states
- 24/7 SOC and Service Desk; ISO recertification; Integrated Management System
- **20+ awards in two years:** NET5.5G Pioneer Award, inaugural AI Award, Best IT Service Provider of the Year 2025 (International Standard Excellence Awards), 1st overall 2025 FG Website Performance Scorecard, BPSR Website Performance Award, Nigeria @ 65 Independence Kitty overall winner
- Executive team: Olusegun Olulade (Customer Centricity & Marketing), Muhammed Sani Ibrahim (Finance & Corporate Services), Olumbe Akinkugbe (Digital Exploration & Technical Services)
- GBB marked 20 years in 2026
